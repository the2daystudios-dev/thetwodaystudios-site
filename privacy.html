Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

2 Day Studios ("we", "us") makes reels, posts, ads and websites for brands. This page explains what we collect when you enquire about our services, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you message us on Instagram or Facebook, answer our chat questions, comment on our posts with a keyword, or fill in the form on this website, we may collect:

  • your name
  • your phone or WhatsApp number
  • your brand name or Instagram handle
  • what you need from us (for example reels, posts or a website) and anything else you choose to tell us

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry. That means contacting you on WhatsApp, Instagram, phone or email to share samples, a quote and next steps. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone outside our team except the tools we use to run the business (see below).

Where it is stored

Enquiries are stored in our private Google Sheet and in our Meta (Instagram and Facebook) inbox, which only our team can access. Meta's and Google's own privacy policies apply to their services.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to follow up and serve you. If you do not become a client, we delete them within 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your details at any time. Message us on Instagram at @thetwodaystudio or email the2daystudios@gmail.com, and we will do it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
